# Capabilities and user-facing help

When asked for help, explain the actions below in the user's language. Present
only the detail needed for their goal. Availability depends on the installed HDS
edition and the agent's file, terminal, browser or native API access.

| Goal | What the skill directs | Example request |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create a Master | Initialize a new native collection and prepare a reusable library | "Buat Master baru untuk channel home berbahasa Spanyol." |
| Extend a library | Reuse suitable profiles and references; add channels or stock | "Tambahkan 12 channel tanpa mengubah stok lama." |
| Research and curate | Separate title demand, content visuals, packaging and narration | "Cari referensi decor timeless dengan narasi yang kuat." |
| Prepare a wave | Allocate titles, profiles, collection names, storage and execution order | "Siapkan 50 judul per channel dan pisahkan koleksi berdasarkan bahasa." |
| Execute or resume | Use native HDS to create briefs/plans and complete the requested stages | "Lanjutkan koleksi ini sampai gambar dan thumbnail selesai." |
| Inspect and hand off | Report evidence, preserve current work and prepare a destination device | "Apa yang kurang dan koleksi mana yang bisa dibawa ke Mac lain?" |

For first use, identify the installation, user's goal and relevant collection.
Ask for missing inputs together when useful. Do not make users fill every
parameter before beginning independent research or explaining a plan.

Offer the optional distinction between "create from scratch", "reuse a supplied
Master/starter", and "execute an existing collection". Do not require a Master
for the last option.

The public package contains reusable procedure and a read-only diagnostic helper.
It does not bundle somebody else's channel stock, images, account settings,
Private engines or a ready-made Master.
